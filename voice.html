TAKE PART IN THE RESEARCH

Your perspective becomes part of the picture.

The People's Voices begins with a simple idea: young people deserve to be asked about the world they are growing up in.

Through the Youth Voice Index, VOISA gathers responses from young people and brings those individual experiences together to understand larger patterns in how youth experience their schools, communities, rights, opportunities, and ability to participate.

One response is one perspective. Thousands of responses can become research.

The Youth Voice Report.

VOISA RESEARCH ANNUAL
02

What if we stopped guessing what young people think — and actually asked them?

The Youth Voice Report turns the perspectives gathered through the Youth Voice Index into a larger body of research. Instead of looking at one experience at a time, VOISA brings responses together to identify patterns, understand differences, recognize concerns, and see where young people believe change is needed.

The research is presented through data, statistics, visualizations, written analysis, and youth-generated ideas. The goal is not simply to collect numbers. It is to understand what those numbers are telling us.

Each annual report creates another piece of the larger record — allowing us to look at youth perspectives over time and ask a bigger question: are we actually becoming better at listening?

YOUR VOICE DOESN'T END HERE

Research can show us where we are.

But understanding a problem is only the beginning. Young people have the ability to participate in their communities, ask questions, organize around issues they care about, learn how systems work, and help create the changes they want to see.

Your voice does not become less important when the survey ends. Take what you learn, bring it into your community, and do not be afraid to participate.

Your voice is not just
something to be heard.
It is something to be used.
