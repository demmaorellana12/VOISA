01 · THE ARCHIVE

THE PEOPLE’S PAGES

The People’s Pages is VOISA’s digital youth magazine and growing archive of words, ideas, art, and perspectives.

It is a place for young people to say what they need to say, to create without unnecessary constraints, explore ideas without being told what they should think, and share the parts of themselves that deserve to be heard.

Every edition centers around a different theme, interpreted by young people in their own way. Poetry, essays, stories, artwork, photography, research, music, and more can become part of the Pages.

Because your voice does not need to wait for permission to matter.

This is your page. What will you put on it?

02 · THE CURRENT EDITION
EDITION 001

BECOMING

What does it mean to become? To become someone with your own opinions, your own truths, and your own beliefs, beliefs shaped by your own morals rather than borrowed from the world around you. To become yourself in your purest form. But what does becoming look like when the world is constantly telling you who to be? Becoming under the influence and pressure of today’s opinions. Becoming because someone else decided who you should be. Becoming because it is easier to conform than to question. For our first edition, we want to know what becoming means to you. Maybe it means growing up, changing your mind, finding your voice, losing something, discovering something, or becoming someone you never thought you would be.

There is no single way to become. There is no single way to create.

Maybe becoming means realizing that you are allowed to question what you were taught. Maybe it means choosing your own beliefs instead of inheriting someone else’s. You don’t have to interpret the theme literally. Becoming is not simply about who you are; it is about who you choose to become and what the person you become chooses to leave behind. Your beliefs shape your choices. Your choices shape your actions. And your actions shape the world you leave behind.

-Demma Orellana, Founder and Director of VOISA

04 · BEFORE YOU SUBMIT

THE GUIDELINES

We want your work to be yours.

For Edition 001, submissions should:

  • Connect meaningfully to the theme BECOMING.
  • Be your original work.
  • Be appropriate for a youth publication.
  • Respect the privacy, rights, and dignity of other people.
  • No usage of AI.
  • Be something you are comfortable having published publicly.
  • Any prose or research may be up to 3,500 words.
  • Any audio submissions may be up to 7 minutes.
  • Any video submissions may be up to 5 minutes.
  • Review the appropriate format for your work on the offical submission form.
That’s it.

You don’t have to follow a certain style. Your poem doesn’t have to rhyme. Your artwork doesn’t have to look a certain way. Your perspective doesn’t have to agree with everyone else’s.

We give you the theme. You decide what it means.

05 · FROM THE FOUNDER

A SAMPLE PAGE

FROM THE FOUNDER

Every theme can be interpreted a thousand different ways.

Here is one interpretation of BECOMING, written by VOISA’s founder, Demma Orellana.

Your piece does not have to sound like this.
It does not have to look like this.
It only has to sound like you.

I Haven't Met All of Me Yet
What a thought.
I haven’t met all of me yet.
Some parts are still hiding
behind years I haven’t lived,
places I haven’t been,
people I haven’t loved and lost.
There’s a version of me
who speaks softer,
because she’s been hurt.
And one who speaks louder,
because she finally learned
not to be quiet.
There’s a me who sings in the car
with the windows down,
with a licence I haven't gotten yet.
A me who laughs too hard,
with someone I have yet to meet.
I’ve only met the beginning of myself
the early chapters,
the prologue,
the pencil sketch.
And that’s okay.
Growth isn’t a sudden arrival.
It’s a slow becoming.
A gentle unfolding.
One day,
I’ll look in the mirror
and recognize someone
I’ve never seen before
and she’ll still be me.
So I want to thank myself now.
For being the one my future self
relied on to one day become
a story,
a painting,
a person.
